Twinfold – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/twinfold/issues and include your iPhone model, iOS version, and what happened.

Restore your purchase

Open the Unlock Pro screen and tap Restore purchase. Use the Apple ID that bought Pro.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
