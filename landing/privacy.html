Twinfold – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Data we collect

Twinfold does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (solved puzzles, streak, best times) is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to us. Puzzles are generated on your device.

In-app purchase

The optional "Twinfold Pro" purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your payment details or personal information.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/twinfold/issues.
